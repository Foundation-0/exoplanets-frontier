# Methods, Technical Bottlenecks, and Convergent Resolutions

This document captures the algorithmic architecture, verification standards, failed or stalled technicalities, and convergent solutions developed in `Foundation-0/exoplanets-frontier`. Every method is documented at the point of implementation so collaborating researchers and automated workflows across `Foundation-0` can extend the work without re-deriving dead ends.

## 1. Epistemic and Verification Protocol

Following the five checking rules established in `Foundation-0/astr0`, every search and vetting module enforces five operational gates before any signal is promoted:

1. **Falsifiable predictions**: Every candidate ephemeris predicts exact future transit windows, durations, and depths tested blindly on held-out TESS sectors (Sectors 106 and 107).
2. **Known-answer benchmark recovery**: New detrending and pre-whitening algorithms must first recover published benchmark planets (such as rocky sub-Earth TOI-540 b beneath a 1.5 percent 0.726-day starspot wave on `TIC 200322593`) before running on uncatalogued stars.
3. **Empirical null tests**: Search pipelines run identically on time-reversed and sector-permuted flux series to verify a zero empirical false-alarm rate prior to claiming new detections.
4. **Independent re-measurement**: Signals found in Pre-search Data Conditioning Simple Aperture Photometry (`PDCSAP_FLUX`) are re-measured independently in raw Simple Aperture Photometry (`SAP_FLUX`) and pixel-level Full-Frame Image (`FFI`) or Target Pixel File (`TPF`) cutouts.
5. **Plain epistemic labels**: All claims in `CLAIMS.md` carry explicit tags (`VERIFIED`, `FALSIFIED`, `CONJECTURE`, `KNOWN`) paired with SHA-256 artifact hashes in `results/convergence_certificates.json`.

## 2. Core Pipeline Architecture (Phase 1 Baseline)

- **Two-Stage Alias-Resolving Box Least Squares (`bls_search.py`)**: Across multi-year TESS baselines (2018 to 2026, $\Delta T \approx 2,900\text{ d}$), a naive fine-frequency grid requires over $10^6$ trial periods per star, while a coarse grid misses narrow 1-hour transits due to cycle-count aliasing across year-long observation gaps. Stage 1 runs a dense frequency search on the single highest-cadence 60-day window to isolate top period seeds; Stage 2 expands fine alias combs ($\Delta f \sim 1 / (2 \Delta T)$) around each seed across all sectors, penalizing red noise via the Pont time-correlated noise factor $\beta$.
- **Eight-Gate Astrophysical Vetter (`vetter.py`)**: Requires Pont-corrected Multiple Event Statistic $\text{MES}_{\text{corr}} \ge 9.5$, Signal Detection Efficiency $\text{SDE} \ge 7.5$, physical planet radius $0.45 \le R_p \le 8.0\,R_\oplus$, Keplerian duration consistency $0.20 \le T_{14}/T_{14,\text{circ}} \le 2.6$, odd-even eclipse depth agreement ($< 2.8\sigma$), inverted-flux symmetry rejection, secondary eclipse exclusion across phases $[0.18, 0.82]$, cross-sector `SAP`/`PDCSAP` depth concordance, multi-sector presence ($\ge 2$ sectors), and Gaia DR3 Renormalized Unit Weight Error (`RUWE <= 1.4`) plus radial velocity scatter (`RV_err <= 5 km/s`).

## 3. Promising Directions That Stalled on Technicalities and How Convergence Resolves Them

During Phase 1 execution across 39 held-out Sector 106 and 107 blind candidate pairs and 156 multi-sector dwarf searches, four promising directions hit concrete technical bottlenecks. Examining how those failures connect to sibling `Foundation-0` efforts (`gaia-dr4`, `lost-eclipses`, `cosmic-denoising`, and `certified-orbits`) yielded four convergent solutions implemented in `despot.py`, `ttv_track.py`, `blend_gaia.py`, `converge_io.py`, and `converge.py`.

### Technicality 1: Ephemeris Phase-Drift Clipping Over 8-Year Baselines (`+-60 min` Wall)
- **What stalled**: In Phase 1 blind testing (`results/blind_verification_s106_s107.csv`), nine Sector 106 and 107 tests returned `LOW_SNR_INCONCLUSIVE` or `MARGINAL_RECOVERY`, while several `Tier C` candidates showed suppressed fixed-ephemeris depths. Inspection of the timing offset column (`dt_min`) revealed that `TIC 166848769` (`-58 min` in S106, `-48 min` in S107), `TIC 332644900` (`+56 min` in S106, `+58 min` in S107), `TIC 321011164` (`-60 min` in S107), `TIC 206666344` (`+60 min` in S106), and `TIC 7780510` (`-60 min` in S106) were pressed against the hard `+-60 min` boundary of `blind_check_sector`. Over 1,000 to 2,000 orbits between Cycle 1 (2018) and Sector 107 (2026), a period error of just 4 seconds shifts transit midpoints by over 100 minutes, moving the transit completely outside the predicted window and causing multi-year linear stacks to smear out real planets. Meanwhile, `TIC 70557792` reversed sign from `-20.0 min` (S106) to `+10.0 min` (S107).
- **Convergent resolution (`ttv_track.py`)**: Extract sector-stacked and per-transit midpoints across all historical TESS sectors (2018 to 2026, combining SPOC 2-minute light curves with baseline-spanning `Tesscut` FFI cutouts) using a wide `+-240 min` matched-box timing scanner. Evaluate four nested physical models via Bayesian Information Criterion ($\Delta\text{BIC}$): published constant ephemeris, refined linear period ($P_0 + \Delta P$), quadratic orbital decay or nodal precession ($\frac{1}{2} (dP/dE) E^2$, linking to `Foundation-0/lost-eclipses`), and sinusoidal dynamical Transit Timing Variations ($A \cos(2\pi E / P_{\text{sup}}) + B \sin(2\pi E / P_{\text{sup}})$). Restacking transits along the refined ephemeris rescues phase-smeared candidates and enables clean transit masking for residual multi-planet sibling searches.

### Technicality 2: Dilution-Induced Centroid Pull and Unpropagated High-Proper-Motion Neighbors
- **What stalled**: Two `eumemic/exoplanets` candidates labeled `likely nearby FP` (`TIC 115692561` at $P = 0.850322\text{ d}$ and `TIC 253923533` at $P = 1.156013\text{ d}$) transited cleanly on schedule in Sectors 106 and 107 (`SNR = 4.26` and `3.46` for `TIC 115692561`; `SNR = 3.20` for `TIC 253923533`). In 21-arcsecond TESS pixels, a static nearby star pulls the out-of-transit aperture photocenter toward the neighbor. During a genuine on-target transit, the difference-image centroid stays on the target star, creating an apparent offset relative to the diluted out-of-transit photocenter and triggering false blend rejections. Conversely, unpropagated J2015.5 catalog coordinates misplace high-proper-motion stars by several arcseconds by 2026.
- **Convergent resolution (`blend_gaia.py`)**: Query `gaiadr3.gaia_source` within 65 arcseconds, propagate every source from `J2016.0` to each sector's exact observation epoch via proper motions (`pmra`, `pmdec`), and project coordinates through the sector FITS World Coordinate System (`WCS`). Register the pixel frame by matching the observed out-of-transit photocenter to the flux-weighted Gaia scene model, eliminating static dilution bias. Measure inverse-variance-weighted multi-sector difference centroids in celestial sky coordinates directly against the target and every Gaia neighbor while computing the minimum eclipse depth each neighbor would require (`req_eb_depth > 1.0` physically rules out faint neighbors). This prototype also provides executable blend-audit code for `Foundation-0/gaia-dr4` ahead of the 25 November 2026 ESA archive maintenance window.

### Technicality 3: Active M-Dwarf Rotational Starspot Blinding vs. Filter Attenuation
- **What stalled**: All 19 triggers with $\text{MES} \ge 9.5$ in our initial M-dwarf campaign (`results/discovery_triggers_vetted.csv`, led by `TIC 31281821` at $\text{MES} = 369.8$, $P = 1.06671\text{ d}$) locked onto coherent 0.27-to-1.1-day sinusoidal starspot waves or contact binaries. A 1.0-day running median leaves sub-day spot waves intact so BLS saturates on stellar rotation, whereas narrowing the median window below 0.3 days erases 1-hour planetary transits.
- **Convergent resolution (`despot.py`)**: Per continuous spacecraft orbit segment, identify coherent rotational frequencies via periodograms and fit 4-harmonic Fourier series ($f_{\text{rot}}, 2f_{\text{rot}}, 3f_{\text{rot}}, 4f_{\text{rot}}$) using asymmetric Huber iteratively reweighted least squares (`r < -2.2 * MAD` masked). Downward planetary transits receive zero weight during harmonic estimation, stripping 1 to 3 percent starspot waves by over an order of magnitude while preserving over 99 percent of narrow box-transit depth on synthetic TOI-540 tests.

### Technicality 4: Cross-Sector Raw `SAP_FLUX` Baseline Jumps and Silent Figure NaNs
- **What stalled**: Concatenating unnormalized raw `SAP_FLUX` across sectors with differing pixel apertures caused spurious `sap_mismatch` flags (`TIC 31281821`), and NaN slices in `np.round(...).astype(int)` aborted figure generation in early runs.
- **Convergent resolution (`mdwarf_hunt.py`, `blend_gaia.py`)**: Normalize `SAP_FLUX` per sector with a local running baseline (`norm_sap`), enforce finite-array masks prior to integer orbit casting, and assert non-empty `results/figures/*.png` outputs directly in CI workflows.
